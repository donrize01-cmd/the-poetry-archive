Poem 07

I'LL STAY HERE TONIGHT

I KNOW WHAT I'VE DONE
YOU KNOW WHAT I'VE DONE
I'LL SAY WHAT I'VE DONE
I KNOW WHAT I'VE DONE
NOW YOU KNOW WHAT I'VE DONE

BECAUSE THE TRUTH IS-
I'M NOT DREAMING ABOUT YOU, GIRL
I'M DREAMING ABOUT HER, GIRL
AND I KNOW YOU'RE THE GIRL IN MY BED
AND I'M NOT DREAMING ABOUT YOU, GIRL

IT BREAKS MY HEART, IT'S MY FAULT, I KNOW
NOW YOU'LL HATE ME FOREVER
I CAN'T CONTROL MY HEART
I WRITE THESE POEMS WISHING I COULD
BUT NOW YOU'LL HATE ME FOREVER

I PROMISE YOU I HAD FEELINGS FOR YOU
I DID LOVE YOU FOR A TIME
BUT SHE'S EVERYTHING TO ME
EVEN THOUGH YOU CRADLED ME
WHEN I DID LOVE YOU FOR A TIME

I KNOW WHAT THIS IS
I'LL STAY HERE TONIGHT
ON THESE COLD STREETS
NEVER FORGIVING MYSELF
SO I'LL STAY HERE TONIGHT

YOU DESERVED TO KNOW THE TRUTH
MY HEART IS NOT WITH YOURS
SHE WON'T EVER LOVE ME
BUT YOU CAN'T EITHER NOW THAT YOU KNOW
WHY MY HEART IS NOT WITH YOURS
