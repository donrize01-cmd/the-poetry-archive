Poem 02

KEEPAWAY

KIND ENOUGH TO LET ME DOWN EASY
EASY ENOUGH IT DOESN'T HURT YOU
EASY ENOUGH THAT YOU DON'T CARE
PETRIFIED ENOUGH, I CAN'T FIGHT BACK

ALL IS WELL BUT WON'T END WELL
WHERE ARE YOU RIGHT NOW, BABY?
ALL IS WELL, WE WON'T END WELL
YOU WON'T COME BACK NOW BABY
