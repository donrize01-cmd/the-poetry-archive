Canary Love

Poem 02

Flames

The flames in my heart are burning
They'll char my insides,
They'll burn for you
It's alright, I'll be okay
My heart will tear from the inside out
Your beauty is too much to bear
And the flames are on me bare
There's something here for us
you and I are set aflame
And you and I will burn together
