Poem 19

LOVE YOURSELF: YOU'RE WANTING ME NOW

I'M RUNNING AWAY
FROM THE GLASS OF OUR SHATTERED HEARTS
THEY'RE CUTTING ME UP
I COULDN'T TAKE IT
I DROPPED

WHEN I FELL, I KEPT FALLING
IT WAS BOTTOMLESS, YET
I WAS UNMOVING
STARING AT THE SKY
I TRIED REACHING OUT
BUT THERE WAS NOTHING
MY HEAD HURTS
MY EYES ARE WATERING
AND I'M FALLING FOR YOU AGAIN

YOU'RE EVIL AND BEAUTIFUL
AND EVERYTHING I'VE EVER WANTED
I STARTED MOVING FORWARD
BUT YOU'RE WANTING ME NOW
AND I DON'T KNOW WHAT TO DO
AND I'M STILL FALLING
AND I FELL INTO WATER

BONES BREAKING
BRIDGE COLLAPSING
HEART SHATTERING
I'M TURNING TO SMITHEREENS
I'M TURNING INTO SOMETHING
I'M BECOMING SOMETHING
I'M ENCHANTED BY YOUR LOVE
