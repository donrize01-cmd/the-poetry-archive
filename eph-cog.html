Cog

Breaking away from my love
I feel my heart bending
My soul breaking
I feel my brain contorting
Trying to fit the machine
This is not the type of man I am

But I fear it's the man I'm becoming
As I leave everyone behind
And become a cog in the machine
I feel my bones breaking
Trying to fit the machine
I feel my heart bleeding out
Trying to fit the machine

But I won't let that happen
I will take it all back
I will forge the path to victory
Then my neck snapped
Trying to fit the machine

This can't be how it ends
