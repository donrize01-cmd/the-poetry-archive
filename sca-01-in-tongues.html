A Short Collection for my Affection

Poem 01

In Tongues

When we're speaking in tongues like this
And I'm reminded of how beautiful you are
And I'm grateful to have known you
I know I love you

When we're speaking in tongues like this
Holding each other close, closer than most
Admiring each other so
I'll never let go when we're

Speaking in tongues like this
And loving you so
