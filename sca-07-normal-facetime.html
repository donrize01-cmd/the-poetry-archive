Poem 07

This is a Normal FaceTime Where Nothing Erotic Happens

If I could reach through my phone
I'd surely cup your cheek
Kiss your hand and tell you I love you
Maybe be jealous of the boys you like
Even if they're not real

But you've got all your clothes on
So there's nothing to admire, right?

But the truth is, I admire all of you
No matter what you do or don't have on
I'll admire your pictures in my hidden photos
And I'll admire your smile
Your cute cheeks
Your arms

This isn't like before
It's a normal facetime
But I can't take my eyes off of you
I can't stop blowing kisses at you
Sticking my tongue out at you
I can't stop admiring you
