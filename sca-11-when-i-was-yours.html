Poem 11

When I Was Yours

When I could tell from right and wrong
When I could make choices for myself
When I could do my laundry
When I could do my homework
When I could dream about you at night
My nightmares
When I could make choices for myself
When I was yours

Something bad is happening to me
This indescribable, inescapable feeling
It's like the oxygen is being sucked out of me
Why is this happening to me

I can barely see it
Something in the distance
Some sort of temple
There has to be answers there

I feel like I can see something below me
Visages of dead men breathing
Stomachs split apart, still breathing
Spines curling, resting, sleeping
Hands hold pens with the ink of their tragedies
They write to this day
As I approach Fate

I need a word with Her
There's something I have to say
Because above this declaration
Lay the list of things I can't even remember
I can't even remember what it's like to be hers
Fuck, I feel like crying
But if I cry now, I'll end up just like them
Writing pointless tragedies with crushed bones

I can't even remember what it's like to be yours
