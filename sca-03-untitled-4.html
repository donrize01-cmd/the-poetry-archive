A Short Collection for my Affection

Poem 03

Untitled-04-11.17.26-7.50

But the sun's setting now
Forty-six and thirty-two until
And I'm holding your hand now

Sighhhhh
Aren't you the prettiest flower
The most pretty little thing
I could admire you forever

The touch of silk
And yet comparable to an ocean's breeze
I can almost feel my hair blowing in the wind
And when I kiss your hand
The scent of your perfume is overwhelming
Like an explosion unto me
And when it's your lips
It's a pleasant glue I'll wipe off
With my hand or with my tongue
Before going in for another kiss

When I hold you
I could fall asleep standing
Mi bebé...
