Poem 21

I'M DREAMING ABOUT YOU, GIRL, AGAIN

IM MY DREAM,
A WHOLE LIFE BETWEEN US OCCURRED
BUT I ONLY CAUGHT A GLIMPSE
A SACRED MOMENT BETWEEN YOU AND I
STANDING ON A RIVERBED
LEANING ON THE FENCE
WATCHING THE WATER DRIFT BY

YOU LOOK SO BEAUTIFUL UNDER MOONLIGHT
I ONLY WANT TO KISS YOU ONCE
I'D BE HAPPY WITH THAT
BUT IN MY DREAM, I WAS GREEDY
I STOOD BEHIND YOU
I WRAPPED MY ARMS AROUND YOU
I LEANED MY HEAD DOWN
AND YOU LEANED YOURS BACK

IT HAD TO HAVE BEEN THE BEST MOMENT OF MY LIFE
I HELD ON TIGHTLY
NEVER WANTING TO LET GO
I NEVER WANT TO LET YOU GO
I WANT TO STAY HERE FOREVER
BUT EVENTUALLY, I HAVE TO WAKE UP

THE TRUTH IS, WHEN I WOKE UP
I WAS DEVASTATED
I WAS RIPPED FROM THE LIFE
I HAD ALWAYS WANTED
NOW, THERE'S NOTHING FOR ME HERE
EXCEPT YOU, VALERIE...
I'M SORRY FOR WHAT IT IS
MY ENCHANTED HEART IS ABOUT TO DO
TO YOUR LOVELY SOUL
