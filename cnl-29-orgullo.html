Canary Love

Poem 29

Orgullo

When I met you, I never expected this to happen
You were a queen,
Practically a goddess among women
Your beauty...
It was unlike anything I'd ever seen before
And there was something about you
Something about your demeanor
Something about your voice
I was drawn to you, like metal to magnets
From the start, you reeled me in
From the start, I loved you

My hindsight is clear now
It was your pride
I've always been anxious
I don't look the part
I don't speak the same
I don't dance the ways
But you inspired me
Your elegance, your beauty
It was captivating
Your dancing was so cute
It was enamoring
Before I knew it
When the night fell
I fell as well
