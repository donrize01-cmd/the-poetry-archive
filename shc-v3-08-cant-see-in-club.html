Poem 08

CAN'T SEE SHIT IN THE CLUB

REGRET IS A VOICEMAIL I DIDN'T REALIZE I WAS RECORDING
CALLING YOU WHILE WE'RE AT THE CLUB
IT'S SO DARK IN HERE, HARD TO SEE IN HERE
I THOUGHT WE WERE TOGETHER, BUT THEN I LOST YOU
I WAS SO FRUSTRATED, WHEN I HAD CALLED YOU
I POURED OUT ALL MY HATRED
IT'S SO DAMN HARD TO SEE IN HERE
IT'S SO DAMN DARK IN HERE
WHEN I FINALLY FIND YOU IN THE CLUB
CAN'T SEE SHIT IN THE CLUB
