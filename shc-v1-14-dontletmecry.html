Poem 14

DONTLETMECRY

I looked you in the eye and
wanted to wash away my
tears but I couldn't. I
couldn't bare to with
you watching me. I wanted
you to know that everything
is okay, but without you all I
can do is cry. I look at that
photo we took and all I can
do is cry. I look you in the
eye and all I can do is cry.
Don't let me cry. I can't
bare to cry. I want to be
happy again but all I can
do is cry. I want to be
myself again, to do whatever
with you again, to have you
back again, go dancing or
laughing and smiling again,
but either way I'd let my
tears fall like tsunami waves
crashing into us and I don't
want to do that but you just
let me cry. Why did you let
me cry. I wanted to fix things
I think I remember wanting to
fix things with you but then
I got scared because you don't love me.
