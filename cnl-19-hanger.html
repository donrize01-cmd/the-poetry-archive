Canary Love

Poem 19

Hanger

I found the courage
But why is it so hard in the moment?
I thought this was what I wanted
But letting myself go like this
I feel like a disappointment
But my vision is going blurry
and my nails are getting bloody
I'm losing my air
And I succumb to my fate
