Poem 11

Feelings That I Felt Before the Morning

I feel happy
I feel sad
I feel sleepy
I feel scared
I feel anxious
I feel embarrassed
I don't want to see you tomorrow
I don't want to screw this up
Anymore than I already have
So I feel scared
And I feel anxious
Because I feel depressed
Because I feel like I see you
In a certain light
That you don't see me in
