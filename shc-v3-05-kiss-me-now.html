Poem 05

Can't You Just Kiss Me Now

I know you and I don't mix
And we have lives outside of this
But my feelings and condemnation
Are as inevitable as the tears
That will fall on your face once I confess
That you're not just a friend to me
So just this once, won't you humor me
And just love me back? Just this once,
Can't you just kiss me now?
