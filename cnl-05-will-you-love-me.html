Canary Love

Poem 05

Will You Love Me When I've Gone?

And it's an overbearing feeling
I realize the Earth, as we know it
It's shattering around us
You and I are no house sparrow
We're lovebirds, doves,
And you're my canary, darling

But just for this moment,
You and I can't burn together
And the promise I delivered to you by hand
I must break it to save myself
I can't help but wonder
Will you love me when I've gone?

When your wings regenerate
Come find me, Canary
And we can go back to discovering the universe

Until then, burn for me, baby
I'll collect the ashes of your feathers
And I'll keep them safe until you've come to me
