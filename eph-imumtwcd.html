Okay
Are you doing okay?
I hope you're doing okay

I miss you more than words can describe

All I want right now is to hear your voice again
On a late night call ranting again
Tell me you hate me, or that you missed me
Talk to me every once in a while
Or have you forgotten me?
Because I haven't forgotten you

I miss you more than words can describe

It's been so long
But I'd never forget you
Dear, B.C.

I miss you more than words can describe
