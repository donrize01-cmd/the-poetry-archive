Canary Love

Poem 23

HOPELESS ROMANTIC FANTASIES

HELL IS US IF WE DECIDE TO LET THIS GO
OVER OUR HEADS
PARALYZING US
ENDING US
LESSER MEN EXITING THE BLACKBOX
ENTERING IS THE MYTHOS ITSELF
SAVIOR, I WILL BECOME
SALVATION, I WILL LEAD US TO

ROMANTICIZING MY HATRED
OMNISCIENT FOR A MOMENT
MANY A MEN HAVE FAILED YOU
AND I DON'T WANT TO BE ONE OF THEM
NOR DO I WANT TO DIE WITH YOU
THIS PLACE IS FOR SINNERS
IF I KNOW ANYTHING ABOUT US
CHARRED SKIN IS SOMETHING ONLY I DESERVE

FETCHING OUR HOPELESS ROMANTIC FANTASIES
AND PLAYING IT OUT IN MY HEAD
NEVER DID I IMAGINE US ON TOP OF THE WORLD
THE WAY WE WILL BE WHEN WE REACH THE TOP
ANALYZING THE STARS FROM WAY BELOW
SKY GROWING BLUER THE HIGHER WE CLIMB
INSIDE OUR HEARTS, WE KNOW THE TRUTH
EVERYONE ELSE KNOWS AS WELL
SHE'S THE ONE FOR ME
