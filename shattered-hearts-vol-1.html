About This Volume

Bright nights are so dark.

The Shattered Hearts Collection Volume I: NIGHTS is an introspective psychological drama featuring contemporary poetry that tells the story of The Traveler, who searches desperately for answers, hope, and a way back to the person he can't seem to let go of.
