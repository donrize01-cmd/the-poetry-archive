The Flower I Still Think About

I may never see you again
But you're the flower I still think about
I may never hear your voice
I may never meet your eyes
I may never show you this
But you're the flower I still think about

You're the pretty flower I remember
You're the pretty flower I want to talk to now
But you're the pretty flower that never said a word
And you may be the prettiest flower of all
But you never allowed me to care for you

You can tell me you want nothing to do with me
Pretty flower, I'll understand
You're something and I'm nothing
Pretty flower, I understand
Why you don't remember my name
