A Short Collection for my Affection

Poem 10

Bouquet

I awoke from another nightmare again

The day goes by quickly
Nothing of note, of importance

Laying in my bed
Sighing softly
You can barely hear it
Yet, I feel it in the back of my mind
Maybe I... misplaced something?
I don't know...

But as I rest my eyes, suddenly I see it
Withering feathers all around me
I'm gasping for air
Vision flickering between petals and present
And finally, I remember it

Shit, I have a date tonight!!!!

No laundry done
No clean car
And... ugh, I could use a shower

Quickly finding good clothes to wear
Swiftly cleaning my car out
Admittedly taking a nice, long shower
Thoroughly washing my hair
Using the soap she got me
That she felt matches my "vibe"
Looking at the time
Thinking I have the time!

But I sigh as I approach the picnic blanket
"Look who's 10 minutes late," she said
"I know, I know," I said, "...but I brought you flowers?"
Hesitant, wondering if my bribe would work or not

And it's hours later
We're staring at the moonlight
And between the beautiful site of that and you
"I'm wondering how I got so lucky"
"What?" She said
"I said I..."
