Ephemera

silk & SMOKE (deMO 1)

When the sun hits your face
You look almost like my blood
But you and I are entirely different
Similar only in the fact
That you and I were born this way
Unlike everyone else
Able to spread love around this void
Through our song-like scriptures
And honey-glass tales
Blooming on the riverbeds-
I looked into your eyes
And everything changed.
