Poem 09

INCANTATION (PHASE TWO)

WHERE ARE YOU GOING
WITHOUT ME
HONEYBEE?

OUT THERE SOMEWHERE
GOD KNOWS WHERE
DOING YOUR THING

HELPING PEOPLE
AND PUTTING SMILES ON FACES
LIKE HOW YOU DID WITH ME

BUT I SEE IT STILL
HOW THAT ARMOR YOU
USED TO WEAR

IT'S CALLING YOUR NAME
CHIPPED ON THE ARMOR STAND
BECAUSE OF HIM

IF YOU WEAR THAT ARMOR
YOU'LL BE JUST AS BAD AS HIM
BUT WHAT WOULD I KNOW?

FOR I AM JUST A PASSERBY
