Poem 24

Outro II

And I'll carry myself
The way you wanted me to
I won't disappoint you again
Because I know better than that
So when I look in the mirror
I can say I love you, mom and dad
And I can say I love you, baby
And I can say I love myself

And I don't care that the bridge is gone
And I don't care that I'm always wrong
It's hard to deny my feelings
So I'll accept them and I'll carry myself
The way you wanted me to, girl
When you tell me to stay with you
When you tell me to lay with you
Here in this void, in this new home
Don't get me wrong, I'd never say no
But this time, it's for me
I'd rather be with you than be alone
Even if you don't love me back
