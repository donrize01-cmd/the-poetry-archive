Poem 06

getting over us

i know you wont say it
no point in begging
when i look in your eyes
and they shine with friendship
ill smile and
ill still hug you goodbye
i know what i have to do now
im going to try my best

it feels empty when im not with you
but ill overcome this
and ill come out on top of the Monolith
for the first time, in this void
i believe in myself
i will escape, with or without you
