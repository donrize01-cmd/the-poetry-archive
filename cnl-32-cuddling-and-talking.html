Canary Love

Poem 32

Cuddling and Just Talking and Stuff (Epilogue)

You and I are onto something
You and your Canary Love
You and your elegant touch
But we've been through so much
And we've grown together

There was a dark age to us
But our hearts together are strong
When I'm talking to you
All you want to do is, quote,
"Cuddle and just talk and stuff"
End quote

And a beautiful girl like yourself
When we're cuddling and just talking and stuff
We can show each other the whole world tonight
