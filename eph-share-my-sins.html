SHARE MY SINS

SHARE MY SINS WITH A GHOST THAT HAUNTS ME
SHARE MY SINS WITH THE RUNAWAY
SHARE MY SINS TO A DAUNTING FELLOW
SHARE MY SINS TO FEEL LIKE SHE CARES
SHARE MY SINS TO END THIS GUILT
SHARE MY SINS AND IT WON'T GO AWAY
SHARE MY SINS TO CRY IN YOUR ARMS
BUT I'LL BE SHARING MY SINS ANYWAY
