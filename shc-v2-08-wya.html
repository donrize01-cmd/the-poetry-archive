Poem 08

Wya?

Where are you now?
Heart wrenched open
Everyday I think about you
Replaying memories in my head
Everyday I do that

You were my everything once
Out of all the hearts I could have
Under my enchanted wings

After everything that happened
There was really only one heart I wanted
?
