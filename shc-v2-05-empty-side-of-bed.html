Poem 05

The Empty Side of my Bed

My bed is big enough for two people
But I'm big, the bed is comfortable
But fitting another my size
Would be challenging
The empty side of my bed
Is perfect for a smaller lover
When I look over
On the empty side of my bed
I can think about you
Huffing gently, smiling, snoring
Giving me a goodnight kiss
