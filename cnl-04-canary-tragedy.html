Poem 04

Canary Tragedy

When I'm with you
My heart feels as if it will explode

There's no place capable of properly containing
The feelings that I have for you
The adoration
Because you're the most beautiful canary
I've ever met

When I reach out for your hand
And you reach back
The whole world explodes behind me

Now I have to make up for it
The whole world is screaming our names
Like a child begging for attention
It's up to you, babe
Are we going to answer?

There's a death toll under your name
And under my hands, the blood splatter lay
A vicious tune for our reckoning
In my eyes, I did it all for you
And that's what happens
When a boy like me is obsessed
With a girl like you
