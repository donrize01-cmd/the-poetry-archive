Poem 08

mIND oVER mATTER

i CHOOSE MIND OVER MATTER
i CHOOSE WILL OVER WAYS
wILL OVER WONTS
bECAUSE IM STRONG ENOUGH
tO MAKE MY OWN DECISION
nO ONE CAN LEAD ME
i AM FREE
i CANT BE STOPPED
bECAUSE I CHOOSE MIND OVER MATTER
bECAUSE MY HEART CANT TAKE
aNY OTHER DECISION
bECAUSE I KNOW WHAT HAPPENS
wHEN I CHOOSE DELUSION OVER REALITY
