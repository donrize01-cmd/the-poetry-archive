Poem 12

Fantasy Fanatic

I'm looking at your face right now
I'm looking at your lips right now
I'm looking at your eyes right now
I'm looking at your hips right now
I'm looking at your hair right now
I'm looking at your chest right now
I'm looking at your hands right now
I'm looking at your legs right now

Thinking to myself
Thinking aloud
You've come to check up on me
God, it feels like I'm speaking another language
I can't even breathe right now
All I can think about is you

About caressing your face right now
About kissing your lips right now
About staring into your eyes
And my hands rising up your thighs
About you dancing with me in the night
Grinding your hips against mine
About gently parting your hair
So I can kiss you from behind
About my hands wrapping around your chest
And saying I love you
So you know I'm not lying

I love you
God I love you
And I need you tonight
But it feels like I'm speaking another language
Like I'm speaking in tongues...
