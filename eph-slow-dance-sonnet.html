Ephemera

SLOW DANCING SONNET

YOU AND I AND OUR HANDS INTERTWINED
AND OUR HIPS SWAYING TO THE SONNET
AND YOUR LOVELY CHEEKS PRESSED AGAINST MINE
BEAUTIFUL LIKE A SOARING COMET
AND THERE'S NOTHING LEFT INSIDE THIS ROOM
ASIDE FROM YOU AND I AND OUR LOVE
I KNOW I'LL MEET AN UNTIMELY DOOM
IT'S HELL BELOW OR HEAVEN ABOVE
HERE WE ARE, SLOW DANCING IN THE DARK
GUIDE ME TO HELL WITH YOUR VIVID PRIDE
I'LL LET MYSELF ONTO NOAH'S ARK
YET BEFORE YOU CAME, I NEVER TRIED
IF THERE WAS EVER A DULL MOMENT
TAKE YOUR WINGS, MI AMOR, AND OWN IT
