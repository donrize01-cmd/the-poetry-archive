Canary Love

Poem 33

Canary Fly (Bonus Poem)

I can see her in the distance
She's going somewhere far away

I want to go somewhere far away, too
I want to fly
Go every where and see every thing
Just for a moment

Now I feel as if I'm dying softly
And she's not here to be my lover
If you can hear me, I'd like to go with you

Don't leave me here

Return to Cuddling and Just Talking and Stuff (Epilogue) The End Canary Love
