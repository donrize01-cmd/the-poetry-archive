Poem 09

WhoRU?

When I saw your face in the club
How enchanted I was by your eyes
Omens cursing my heart

After you and I locked eyes
Racing heart, omen's fault
Everything around me dark

You took my hand and danced
Over my past mistakes
Under the collapsed bridge
