Ephemera

Untitled-03-10.25.26-1.10

Look me in the eye and say I mean something to you
There's not a shadow of a doubt in my mind
I know I'm wrong, I know I do
But I can't get the thought out of my head

It's my own fault, the way my brain is wired
The way I'll look at my hands
And let my tears fall anyways

I screamed so loud I felt my throat go sore
I cried so much I couldn't feel my face
I'll stay in my head and keep going to war
Cocaine dreams, I'm still floating in space

Then there's you
But only for a moment
