Poem 24

Outro III

When you reached out your hand for me
I wasn't ready
I wasn't prepared
To reach back

But now we're here
And there's no telling if this'll last
But if it's anything like how I've been writing
Or maybe how everyone's been saying
Or how Valerie is hoping
Then you and I are going to collapse
I don't want it to happen
But it's near unavoidable
Our hearts are nothing but smithereens

But no matter what, I'm still enchanted
By your love, your beauty
Your perfection
You're everything I strived for
Across all these stupid poems
You're the only thing I ever wanted
Even when I moved on
Even when you weren't around
Deep in my brain
I knew I wanted you

And now you're mine
And I'm yours
And we can be enchanted forever
And show the world that they're wrong about us
Or, if this blows to smithereens
We can always just repeat this cycle

Either way, I'll still love you
