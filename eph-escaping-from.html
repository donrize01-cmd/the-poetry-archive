Ephemera

WhAt ArE yOu EsCaPiNg FrOm?

From here on out, it's all about deep breaths
It only gets harder from here
It only gets better from here

You who must not be named
You all who'll never have any names
No more poems
No more hatred
No more anything

This goodbye is all I have to offer you
No, I'm not running
No, I'm not hiding
I'm chasing

I'm chasing down the future that belongs to me
And this is just the push that I need
Every moment in the last eleven months
Has lead me to this, right here, right now
So no, I'm not running
And no, I'm not hiding

Even if I don't know, I do know
Something I said, something I've done
Some employee I've been
Some friend I've been

It's time to put this to rest
It's time to walk away
But just know, I'm not running away
And know, I'm not hiding either
I acknowledge my mistakes
I own up to it
Whatever it is I did

When this is all over
There'll be no smoke when the dust settles
And no regrets to hang out to dry
There'll be nothing

The poems are over now
