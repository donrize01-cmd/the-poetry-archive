Poem 08

Under Thumbs

I see you through screens
I see you in my mind
And if it is such-
What is this despair?

There's something I feel in my hands
It's indescribable
It's inescapable
It's complicated
It hurts and it's ecstasy

When I feel it under my thumb like this
And it reminds me of
And I'm reminded of
I know that

When I'm thinking of you under my thumb like this
Below me and sharing hot breath
Kissing and feeling skin
And I keep feeling it under my thumb

Those nights ago
What was that dream about...
