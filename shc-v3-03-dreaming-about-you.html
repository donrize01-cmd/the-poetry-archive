Poem 03

I'M DREAMING ABOUT YOU, GIRL

IN MY DREAM,
MY WHOLE LIFE WAS ABLAZE
THE HOUSES AROUND MY OWN
SET TO FLAME
AND I HAD ONLY ONE THOUGHT:
BEING INSIDE YOUR ARMS
EVEN THOUGH YOU'RE NOT MINE
I'M DREAMING ABOUT YOU, GIRL

WE WENT TO A CONCERT TOGETHER
WE SAT APART
AN INTERLOPER WONDERS WHY
WE DON'T SIT TOGETHER
I THINK, IN MY DREAM
YOU ALREADY KNEW HOW I FELT

YOU SAID I CALL YOU NAMES
IT MAY BE TRUE, I CAN'T RECALL
YOU MEAN ENOUGH TO ME
TO NOT CALL YOU BY YOUR NAME
YOU MEAN ENOUGH TO ME
FOR ME TO WANT TO HOLD YOU
AND NOT LET GO

THE TRUTH IS, OUTSIDE THE DREAM
THERE'S A GIRL SLEEPING BESIDE ME
AND WE'RE LOVEBIRDS
KISSING AND LOVING
LIKE TWO SOULMATES
SHE LOVES ME
AND YOU DON'T
BUT I'M DREAMING ABOUT YOU, GIRL
AND I FEAR I WON'T STOP ANYTIME SOON
