Canary Love

Poem 16

I'M TIRED, CANARY

This pain in my chest
It's unlike anything I've ever felt before
It feels similar to death;
The isolation is what kills me

Why must we be so far apart?
There's a part of me that wants it to end
But I don't have the strength to do it myself
Of course, that's why you had to do it

But what is wrong with me?
Why am I this way?

I recall the scars in the mirror
The burns I had given myself
And the ashes that fell from my skin
I recall doing it
But I can't recall why

You're not by my side anymore
And I'll wait eons before I see you again
Because you're too amazing for me
You, the singer
You, the talker
You, the Canary

I'm tired, Canary
I'm tired of feeling this way
I'm tired of being trapped, away from you
There's only so much I can do without you
I'm obsessed, you're my only vice
You're killing me, but I don't care
You've already killed me once anyway

Reborn, I'm a slave
I'm on your leash, Canary
There's nothing good about my life
Except you and your love
