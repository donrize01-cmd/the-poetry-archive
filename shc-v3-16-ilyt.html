Poem 16

ilyt

i don't love you anymore

let's keep our feelings to ourselves
on my shoulders lay your burdens
vehemently erasing you from my mind
emancipating myself from your heart

you say you have feelings for me
on my shoulders lay your heart
uprising, I thought your rejection was

then, you just said it
on my shoulders lay your burdens
on my shoulders lay your heart ?
