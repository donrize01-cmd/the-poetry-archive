Poem 04

INCANTATION (PHASE ONE)

PUT A SPELL ON ME
HONEYBEE
FLY AWAY

INTO THE UNKNOWN
OH MY
I WANT TO GO WITH YOU

WEATHERED GLASS
AND BROKEN BONES
CHIPPED BLADE

BUT YOU'RE NO
KNIGHT OF MINE
HONEYED GLASS

IT CONSUMES YOU
BELITTLES YOU
IT WILL SHAPE YOU

IF I HAD GONE WITH YOU
I WOULD'VE SAVED YOU
FROM HIS INCANTATION

BUT I AM JUST A PASSERBY
