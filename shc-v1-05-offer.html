Poem 05

Offer

I give you my offer
Irrefutable
But you decline

It confuses me
I don't know why
You'd decline

The offer
Would've saved you
Changed your life

But you threw it away
How could you do
Such a thing to me?

Worse yet
How could you refuse
Being happy for yourself?
