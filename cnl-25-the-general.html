Canary Love

Poem 25

The General

I wanted to celebrate by having the night with you
But you and I can't go a moment without loving
Loving led to kissing
And it made my blood rush
I want this and you want this

In my bedroom now
People here call me the General
You don't need to worry about that baby
You can call me whatever you want, darling
Whatever makes your own blood rush
As I push your head down into the sheets
The control is thrilling

Tell me what you're feeling
Do you like what I'm doing to you?
I might leave a few marks on you
I hope you admire them as much as I do
As much as I enjoyed leaving them on you
This way, with my fangs engraved on your neck
Everyone knows your mine

I've got your hands all tied up
Do you feel like you're doing this wrong?
Oh, baby, you can't really mess up in here
and as long as I've got my hands on you
We're doing something right
The only thing I want you to think about
Is my hips against yours

This feels bad, doesn't it, but
Even when you're bad, you're good
That's something about you that I love, darling
You've got me going crazy
On top of me now
This ecstasy is beyond measure
My hands where no one else's have been
You're tongue wrapped around my thumb
Pleasure yourself using me, darling
