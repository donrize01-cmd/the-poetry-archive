Canary Love

Poem 01

Onto Something (Prologue)

You and I are onto something
You and your Canary Love
You and your elegant touch
Rising up and down
But we've lost the plot

I'll show you how much you mean to me
All I ask in return is your love
You don't have to change or be perfect
You just have to exist and love me back
It's all I ever wanted from you

An a beautiful girl like yourself
You're onto something being with me
I can show you the whole world tonight
