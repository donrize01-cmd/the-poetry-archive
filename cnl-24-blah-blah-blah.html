Canary Love

Poem 24

BLAH BLAH BLAH (INTERLUDE)

The truth is, I love you
No matter what, I love you
This book is about loving you
It's hard to love you
But I still love you
I hope you know I love you
Don't you know I love you
I love you

I love you more
I love you most
Moster
Mostest
I love you so much I burned everything for you
I love you so much I killed myself for you
I love you so much that I don't want you to think
About the sad things in this book
I love you so much
Whenever you look back to this book
Just skip to the final act
Skip the blah blah blah
And just take my love
