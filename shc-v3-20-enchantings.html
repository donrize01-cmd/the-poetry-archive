Poem 20

ENCHANTINGS

I'M ENCHANTED BY YOUR LOVE
I FEEL MY WHOLE BODY HEATING UP
FROM THE TIPS OF MY TIPPY TOES
TO THE PEAK OF MY HAIR YOU RUFFLED
I CAN'T MOVE
I'M TOO ENCHANTED BY YOUR LOVE

I'M LOST HERE
AND I'M LOSING MYSELF
EVERYTHING THAT'S HAPPENED TO US
AND HELL IS US IF WE CHOOSE TO KEEP DOING THIS
IN THE DISTANCE, I CAN SEE WHERE WE STARTED
THE TRAGEDY
NOW? NOW THERE'S THESE POEMS
AND THEY AREN'T SUPPOSED TO BE ABOUT YOU

WHEN I LOOKED AT THE SKY
I SAW SMITHEREENS

THE SMITHEREENS OF OUR LOVE

YOU AND I WILL NEVER BE
AND I DON'T KNOW HOW YOU KNEW
AND I DON'T KNOW WHAT IT IS YOU FEEL
BUT STOP FEELING IT
END THIS NIGHTMARE FOR BOTH OF US
I DON'T WANT YOU ANYMORE
I DON'T...

BUT IT'S EVERYTHING I'VE EVER WANTED
UNTIL THIS VERY MOMENT
I BATTLED MYSELF FOR YOU
MY OWN FEELINGS AND HEART
I ESCAPED THE VOID WE WERE LEFT IN
I REACHED OUT FOR FREEDOM
I OVERCAME THE MONOLITH
AND I DID IT ALL FOR YOU
ALL SO WE COULD STAY FRIENDS
INSTEAD OF BECOMING LOVERS
BECAUSE I THOUGHT THAT'S WHAT YOU WANTED

BUT TO HAVE LOVED ME ALL THIS TIME?
AND GIRL, IT'S HARD TO DENY I LOVE YOU
AND GIRL, IT'S HARD TO DENY I DREAM ABOUT YOU
BUT GIRL, WE HAVE TO END THIS
IT'S HARD TO DENY I'M ENCHANTED BY YOUR LOVE
BUT WE HAVE TO END THIS
THERE'S NO WAY OUT OF THIS
I KNOW HOW THIS ENDS

AND OF COURSE, LIKE CLOCKWORK
YOU'RE THERE
YOU'RE ABOVE
YOU'RE LOOKING AT ME
I CAN BARELY SEE IT BEFORE IT HITS MY FACE:
YOUR TEAR

BUT WHY
WHY ARE YOU CRYING
AND WHY IS IT YOU CAN'T SEE WHAT I SEE?

THIS WILL DESTROY US
SO WHY AM I STILL REACHING FOR YOU?

THE TRUTH IS, I DON'T CARE

I DON'T CARE ABOUT WHAT HAPPENS TO ME
I DON'T CARE ABOUT WHAT HAPPENS TO VALERIE
I DON'T CARE ABOUT WHAT HAPPENS TO MY PAST
I DON'T CARE ABOUT WHAT HAPPENS IN OUR FUTURE
IF YOU'RE GONNA JUMP
IF YOU'RE GONNA LAND IN MY ARMS
JUST DO IT ALREADY
GOD, I'M SO ENCHANTED BY YOUR LOVE
I FEEL MY EYES GLOWING
MY HEART RACING, MY BODY SWEATING
I FEEL MYSELF PANTING
LOOKING AT YOUR BEAUTY
YOUR FACE, YOUR EYES, YOUR LIPS
YOUR ENCHANTINGS
YOU'RE ENCHANTING ME

I KNOW I'LL KEEP DREAMING ABOUT YOU
I KNOW I'LL KEEP WRITING ABOUT YOU
I KNOW I'LL KEEP THINKING ABOUT YOU
SO JUST COME HERE, TO ME
BE ENCHANTED BY MY LOVE
MY DEDICATION, MY LOYALTY
MY HEART
MY SHATTERED HEART

MY BROKEN HEART
