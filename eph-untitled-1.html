Ephemera

Untitled-01-10.16.26-3.05

I'm going to miss you and your memories
But it's clear to me now
Days have gone by
No response from you now
Why's that possible, how
I won't bow at your feet
For you to loot at my eyes, now

You may find this
And I'm already gone
Maybe, I haven't decided yet
I have, I just regret my decision
Maybe I made a mistake
Somewhere I don't know about

Hindsight makes my vision
So painfully clear sometimes
