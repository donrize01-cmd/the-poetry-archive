Canary Love

Poem 09

HOLDING HANDS

WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM THE HIGH
WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM THE NIGHT
AND NOW WE'RE HOLDING HANDS

WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM THE HIGH
WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM THE NIGHT
AND NOW WE'RE HOLDING THEIR HANDS

WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM SKYSCRAPERS
WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM THE NIGHT
AND WE'RE DRAGGING THEIR BODIES TOGETHER

WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM THE MOON
WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM EACH OTHER
BUT I'LL PUSH MYSELF ONTO YOU ANYWAYS

WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM THE HIGH
WE'RE COMING DOWN FROM THE NIGHT
AND NOW I'M HOLDING YOU
