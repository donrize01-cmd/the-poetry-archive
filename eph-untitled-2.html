Ephemera

Untitled-02-10.18.26-12.17

Never a dull moment
Unless I've spent it alone

But when it's your chest against mine
Your lips against mine
Your hands on mine
It's never a dull moment when you are mine

Do you see me here?
The way I'm looking at you
The way I miss you when you leave
The way I think about you
The way I admire you
Like a beautiful skyline

Never a dull moment
With your breath in my face
Pushing all your weight onto me
That glare in your eye
The way the sun hits your eyes
Like your transferring the sun's rage
Into every bite I feel

I'm ready to keep going
Whenever you're ready
