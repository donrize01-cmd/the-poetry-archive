Canary Love

Poem 15

Books and Rocks and Things to Remember You By

I love you!
And I want to show you
The only way I can properly express it
Is these artificially typed epics, just for you

Here's what reminds me of you:
Dark romance novels
Rocks and gemstones
Poetry
Depression
Obsession
Love

Dark romance novels
They're right up your alley
But I know one of the only reasons you like them
Is because when we're apart, you crave my touch

Rocks and gemstones
The heart and soul of the Earth
You cherish our planet
and that is admirable

Poetry
You're a trying poet, you grow every piece
You can get lost in the art
But so can I

Depression
Sometimes you and I feel this way
But the chokehold you have me in
Truly kills me

Obsession
I'm absolutely obsessed with you
And your body drives me insane
I can't ever get enough

Love
Love is what I feel for you
It's all I can think about
Aside from the hatred I have for myself
