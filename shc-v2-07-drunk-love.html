Poem 07

DRUNK LOVE

WE CAN DRINK TOGETHER
AS LONG AS WE'LL END UP
KISSING TOGETHER
THAT WAY
THE SMELL OF YOUR CIGARETTES
WON'T BE AS POISONOUS TO ME

ANCIENT TALES TOLD ME
YOU AND I WOULD BE FREE
WE CAN ESCAPE TO THAT TOGETHER

IF YOU'LL COME DOWN WITH ME
THE SMELL OF YOUR CIGARETTES
WON'T BE AS HELLISH TO ME

WE CAN LIVE UNTIL WE'RE SMITHEREENS
AS LONG AS YOU'RE BY MY SIDE
NOTHING ELSE MATTERS
SO I'LL WRITE THESE POEMS ABOUT YOU
UNTIL THE SMELL OF YOUR CIGARETTES
BURNS ME ALIVE
