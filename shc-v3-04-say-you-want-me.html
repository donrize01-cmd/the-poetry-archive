Poem 04

Say You Want Me

It's okay if we have nothing real
If that's the case, then just tell me now
Then we can set aside this ordeal
It's not like we ever had a vow
But maybe I thought there was something
You smile whenever I'm around
But between you and I, there's no thing
No feelings of love or else abound
Love, love, it didn't have to be love
And I don't need a romance affair
When we meet, look at me, look above
Say you want me, end this nightmare
Even if it's not true, I don't mind
For a while, you've always been lying
