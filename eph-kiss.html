KISS

A dinner non-date night
Spent with a pretty girl like you
And a sinner like me
Driven by lust

It's not wrong to conclude
I crave your touch
Your whole body, your lips
And I may get my wish tonight

So it's dinner time
At a grill, I'm three years before my time
We're laughing, we're chatting
We're talking about each other's bodies
We're fine dining as if we were one
But we are two

At an ice cream parlor
And I give you an earbud
And you're listening to my music
And you're swaying your hips now
And I take your hands
And before we get to the front of the line
We're dancing, and it's getting dark now

Now we're at a playground
The moon's out, we're closer now
We're dancing again, getting more intimate
I'm holding you close
Your hips are against mine
We're loving the way we move

We're swinging now
Holding hands and taking pictures
But you ask to sit down with me
Still holding hands, you bring mine to your lips
Your soft lips against my hand
That's all it took

It was only moments before we kissed
And after that, my lust
Was much greater than my love
