Poem 07

Nothing or Something

A nothing or a something
I bask in your warmth
Wondering to myself
If it was ever worth it
If this is nothing or something
But I can't figure it out
We're different
Like a cherry blossom versus oak
A beautiful ocean versus puddles
A gorgeous landscape versus home
A stunning woman versus me
She is something and I am nothing
And that is the way it's meant to be
