Poem 23

INCANTATION III

WON'T YOU COME HOME ALREADY
HONEYBEE

BECAUSE I HAD ALMOST
COMPLETELY FORGOTTEN ABOUT YOU
AND I WANT TO SEE YOU AGAIN
I MISS YOU, HONEYBEE

YOU'RE NO CANARY OF MINE
BUT I STILL HAD A PLACE
DEEP INSIDE MY HEART FOR YOU
AND NOW WE'RE NOWHERE TO BE FOUND

LONG AGO, I KNEW YOU
I FELL FOR YOUR INCANTATION
YOUR CHARM, YOUR DELIGHT, YOUR SMILE
BUT THAT ALL FELL APART

SO I KNOW WHAT IT'S LIKE
AND I SEE IT IN YOUR EYES
YOU'RE NOTHING LIKE THAT
NOT AT ALL
YOU'RE JUST FINDING YOUR PEOPLE
AS I AM

I KNOW YOU STILL CARE FOR ME
I STILL HOLD THAT PLACE IN MY HEART
BUT WE HAVE OUR TRUE PLACES
SO IT CAN BE BITTERSWEET
AND HAPPY, TOO
WHEN I FINALLY SEE YOU OFF FOR GOOD

THEN I'LL JUST BE A PASSERBY
