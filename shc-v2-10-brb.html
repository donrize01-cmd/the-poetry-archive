Poem 10

Brb?

BEFORE I LEAVE YOU FOREVER
ENTERING A VOID OF LIGHT

RIGHTEOUSNESS OVERCOMES ME
INITIALLY I HAD DISMANTLED
GAY DREAMS DISSIPATING
HELL IS THE FLAMES ABOVE US
TERRORIZING OUR REALITY TOGETHER

BEFORE I LEAVE US BEHIND
AFTERWORDS PLAGUING OUR THOUGHTS
CHECKING, DOUBLE-CHECKING US
KILLING US AFTER THEY TOY WITH US
?
