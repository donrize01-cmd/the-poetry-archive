He's Playing For Keeps

He's my best friend He was my lover And you all are his best friends But you don't hold his heart close You're not the best thing to ever happen to him And you don't tear him apart There's something about us Something about his lips Something about our childhood memories That neither of us can remember There's something about how He still kisses me Even though we aren't one It's something about the way he touches me And the way he holds me after That makes me wonder what his angle is It's something about this feeling The cage he says he's in It's something about my love for him And his love for me It's something about his angel eyes I can see it now My baby ain't playing He's playing for keeps Wanting to live with me Wanting to touch me Wanting to kiss me He's the best I've ever had But he's the worst
