Poem 15

goosebumps

i got goosebumps from the sight of the monolith
because the tales foretold her greatness
but to this extent were discussions unheld
and the ravages of the land brought
tears to my overwhelming joy
because to this extent were discussions unheld
that she was this perfect goddess
and in her hands hold a little lovestruck boy
and that boy, he must feel what it is that i feel
he must feel in awe, so much so he can't speak
and i understand that feeling very well
because the goosebumps leave me in silence
as the goddess leaves a pentimento behind
