Poem 19

LOVE YOURSELF: I'M LOST WITHOUT YOU NOW

LOVE MYSELF
WITHOUT YOU NOW I CAN'T LOVE MYSELF
LOVE MYSELF
WITHOUT YOU NOW I CAN'T HATE MYSELF
LOVE MYSELF
WITHOUT YOU NOW I DON'T KNOW WHAT I'M DOING
LOVE MYSELF
WITHOUT YOU NOW I DON'T KNOW WHERE I'M GOING
LOVE YOURSELF
YOU'RE AMAZING WITHOUT ME NOW, BABY
LOVE YOURSELF
I'M LOST WITHOUT YOU NOW, BABY
LOVE YOURSELF
WITHOUT YOU NOW I'M NOTHING BUT SMITHEREENS
