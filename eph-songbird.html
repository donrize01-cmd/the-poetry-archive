Ephemera

Songbird

The Songbird is soaring in the trees
She's like a Canary
But she's something more

The Songbird is soaring through the city
Her lyrics are gorgeous
But there's something more

The Songbird is reaching into my soul
Shall I give in-
Shall I let go?

The Songbird is singing a song to me
Her voice is... enchanting
I'm in enchantings

The Songbird is transforming me
She's changing me
She's changing me

The Songbird will blow me
To smithereens
To high hell

The Songbird's cries are on me now
They lay on my shoulders
We're sharing our sins

But I've sung the Songbird's song before
So I'll reach back
And I'll grab her heart
