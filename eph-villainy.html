VILLAINY

VILLAINY, MALICE, AND BAD INTENTIONS

I SAW THE DARKNESS AND NEVER WENT BACK
I FIND THAT THERE'S A PATTERN IN MY WAYS
THERE'S ANCIENT REMNANTS HERE
THEY'RE ALL WATCHING ME

HEAVY IS THE CROWN ON MY HEAD
MY CROWN OF VILLAINY

BABY, I DON'T MEAN TO
BUT FOR NOW, I MUST BREAK YOUR HEART
FOR VILLAINY, MALICE, AND BAD INTENTIONS
DON'T LATCH ONTO ME NOW
THERE'S NOTHING FOR YOU ON THE OTHER SIDE

BUT FOR ME
THERE'S A WHOLE WORLD WAITING FOR ME
BABY, I DON'T MEAN TO
BUT FOR NOW, I MUST FLY AWAY
I MUST LEAVE YOU BEHIND

BECAUSE I NEED THIS
VILLAINY, MALICE, AND BAD INTENTIONS
THEY ONLY GET ME SO FAR
ALL THE RUMORS ABOUT ME ARE TRUE
ALL THE ONES YOU HEARD FROM ME, TOO
BABY, I DON'T MEAN TO
BUT I JUST BROKE MY OWN HEART
AND I CAN'T BE WITH YOU ANYMORE
I NEED TO BE LAID TO REST

BABY I DON'T MEAN TO
BUT HEAVY IS MY CROWN
OF VILLAINY, MALICE, AND BAD INTENTIONS
