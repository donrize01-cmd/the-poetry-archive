Poem 22

SEE YOU AGAIN

I CAN'T EVER LOVE YOU THE WAY I SHOULD
SO WHEN I SEE YOU AGAIN, I HATE IT
YOU KNOW I'D DIE TO LOVE YOU, KNOW I WOULD
WHEREVER IT SENDS ME, I'LL SEE IT FIT
BECAUSE I SAW YOU AND MY HEART FLUTTERED
I DIDN'T EVEN KNOW THAT YOU'D COME BACK
MY HEART SKIPPED A BEAT, MY HEART HAD STUTTERED
I FORGOT HOW MUCH YOU'RE SOMETHING I LACK
BABY, I'VE FALLEN, AND NOW I'M IN NEED
AND I DON'T EVEN TALK TO YOU ENOUGH
YOU'RE SOMEONE I LOVE, I COULD USE YOUR LEAD
BUT IF YOU KNEW, I'D BE MET WITH REBUFF
     SO WE DON'T BELONG, AND I'M IN THE WRONG
     SO I'LL SING THIS SONG, AND WE CAN STAY STRONG
          THE WAY WE ARE
