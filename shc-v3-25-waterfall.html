Poem 25

Waterfall (Bonus Poem)

Ever since that day my heart has known you
It knows everything you like and hate
Your adorations and your fears
They're all mine to know

When I let my tears fall like a waterfall
When I couldn't hold myself together
When we were in the VOID
The car
The alleyway
Now we're home

It's been years since that day
And I feel it slipping away
The memories of before
I feel it fading now

I love you so much
I forgot why I was even sad
And I'm so happy to know
You love me so much
I forgot why I was even...

Why we were blown to smithereens

It's fading now
