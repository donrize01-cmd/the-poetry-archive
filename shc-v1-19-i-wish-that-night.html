Poem 19

I Wish That Night Never Ended

If it were up to me
I'd be smiling forever
Trapped in that moment

I reached out to grab you
And I always second guess
But you always let me
Are you uncomfortable?
Or do you like it
The same way I like it?
The way in which
Your touch, your being close
Provides me comfort

And I never want to hurt you
But your eyes and your smile
The silly faces and giggles
They show me so much
But so does my head

And so does our time apart
Even our times together
You've never been cold to me
But perhaps to the world
You show a certain attitude
And my brain kills me
By saying you hate me

But in that particular moment
My freedom, our freedom
Surrounded by people
I've never seen before
But I took you in my arms
And that smile on your face
When I looked back
At the memory we had created
I'll never forget it

And I'll never forget the smile on mine, either
The biggest, widest smile I've ever had
The most teeth I've ever shown
Your eyes and your smile
It was back in a time
Where you were all mine
But you have other friends
You have other responsibilities
I can't hold you back forever
But I just wish that night never ended
