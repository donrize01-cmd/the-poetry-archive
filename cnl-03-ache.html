Canary Love

Poem 03

Ache

Heartaches and bellyaches
And things that happen to me
Every time I look at you
An evaluation takes place within my soul

I come to find out
There's nothing on this planet
That I love more than you

It comes with a cost
And the pain, of course

The ache of loving you
