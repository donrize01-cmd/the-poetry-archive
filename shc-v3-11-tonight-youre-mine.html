Poem 11

TONIGHT YOU'RE MINE

Darling, as long as there's nothing here
As long as we can forget it in the morning
I want you all to myself tonight

No one can stop me from loving you
Tonight you're mine
So let me wrap my arms around you
And kiss your face gently

Tonight there's nothing here
And tomorrow we'll forget it in the morning
So tonight you're mine

And you can wrap your legs around my waist
And you can kiss me back softly
Your pretty eyes staring into mine
And I nearly fainted at the sight of them

Tonight there's nothing between us
And tomorrow we'll forget it in the morning
So please be mine tonight
