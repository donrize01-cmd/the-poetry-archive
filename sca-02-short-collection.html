A Short Collection for my Affection

Water swaying in the wind
Crashing against beach shores
It reflects wonderfully in your eyes

A painful light
But it highlights your beauty
For once, a star is inferior

Something in my heart is tingling
Skipping around, jumping for joy
Fluttering and spreading pretty wings

Collecting my feelings
Collection for affection
Awh, I love this feeling
