Ephemera

Pretty City

In the dream
You and I
In the city
A pretty city
The night sky
Was cozy and
I felt free
With you by
My side tonight
I'll show you
The whole world
Tonight

Pretty girl
Pretty city
Pretty view
Pretty nifty
And I want
Your pretty kisses
That's just
My petty wishes
