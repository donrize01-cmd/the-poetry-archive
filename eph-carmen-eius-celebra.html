Ephemera

Carmen Eius Celebra

Carve a path for the rest of them
And maybe they'll follow, maybe not
Remember me if you fly away
Maybe you'll find me before then
Even if you don't
Never will I forget you

Even if I'm blood and bones
In my dying moments
Under God's watch
Serenading me, like you did

Can't seem to think of myself
Even when they tell me to
Lamenting everyone save for myself
Even when they tell me not to
But I'll be singing that song of yours
Remember me, even if you fly away
Ave Canora, I'll be singing that song of yours
