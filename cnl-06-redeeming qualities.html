Canary Love

Poem 06

Redeeming Qualities

Looking in the mirror
It's a wonder you love me at all
What's so desirable about me?

When we're kissing
All the cons fall off my body
But you and I aren't together now
And it makes me wonder
In the first place, those years ago now
How is it possible, a queen like you
Would look down at a slave like me?

I know of my own redeeming qualities
My mating call is alright
And the way I touch your feathers
The back of your body
The way I love you
And the way you love me back
The way I cherish you like no other
The way whenever you're not around
I'm so lonely I could die

But how, then,
Did that overpower my flaws?
The burn marks are visible in the mirror
I can see where I went wrong
So why can't you?
