Canary Love

Poem 20

You and I in the Blackbox

Every time you and I have locked eyes
I found beauty behind the madness
Now, there's only pain and tears
I wish we didn't end up this way
But your heart gave in
And my brain gave in

Can't we walk together
Like we always do?

I know there's nothing here, save for it
But if this is the last waking moment I have with you
I love you so much
I know our fate
And I know where we're going
But the truth is,
Even though I was ready for this
I wasn't ready to let go of you

But we're fading now
And there's nothing we can do about it
You and I are in the Blackbox now
Our fates are sealed, like zipped lips
One last kiss goodbye would be helpful
