Poem 18

Let My Tears Fall II

But they told me it wasn't okay
They told me I wasn't okay

But I wrote this poem for you

"Wash away my tears
And bring me back to life
Love me like nobody else
I promise I'll do the same
I don't want to leave
I don't want to be alone
But you broke all your promises
Can't you just love me like you used to?
Or am I the one in the wrong
What did I do?"

All of our photos are in the wind
I can't cry over you anymore
Not after so long
So why do I feel so somber
It's like the tears want to come out
But that concrete wall
It won't let them
So I try to shed these tears for you
But I can't

I miss you
And I hate you
And I think I still love you
And I want to let my tears fall
But I can't
Because I've moved on
Or I thought I did at least
But you still inhibit my thoughts
At least, until I think of her
