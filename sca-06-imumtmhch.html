A Short Collection for my Affection

Poem 06

imumtmhch

When I can't feel your lips
When I can't reach for my phone
And see your pretty face

I miss you more than my heart can handle

When I can't hold onto your thigh
When I can't let our arms intertwine
When I can't take pictures of us
When I can't devote to you my lust

I miss you more than my heart can handle

All I need with you now is a moment alone
Consider a car ride home
Touching bone to bone
Or maybe a sneaking suspicion
And suddenly you're in my house
In a ruffle front blouse
And nothing more needs to be said

I miss you more than my heart can handle
