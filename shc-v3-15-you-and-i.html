Poem 15

YOU AND I CAN STILL BE FRIENDS

Still waking up early for you girl,
I'll go out of my way for you girl,
But just for tonight, don't make this a thing

Picking you up from a party
Inebriated asshole
Getting up at 3 A.M. for you, girl
You get in the car, crying
I don't want another moment with you
I won't touch you or hug you
Even if you may need it

Driving now, and not in silence
If it'll make you feel better,
You can play my songs you like
It's raining tonight
But your tears are likely heavier
Still rolling down your face, except now
There's a smile

Maybe it'd be possible
For you and I to stay friends

I can't help but smile back
I did love you for a while, after all
But that love destroyed me
And I had no time to heal
Before getting in bed with another girl
So I want this to be done with
At your house now
Just get out of my car
