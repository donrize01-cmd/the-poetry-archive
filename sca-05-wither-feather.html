Poem 05

Wither Feather

Heaven's reign is upon my shoulders
When I look up from the weight of the pressure
I'm somewhere I don't recognize

It's hard to see
There's so much to feel here
I feel as if I'll be crushed
I can almost envision it:
My atriums and ventricles
My valves and... my blood
Splattered in an ugly mixture
Of lungs, kidneys, guts and gore

The crumpled femur housing aforementioned blood vessels
Bits of my spinal cord may yet stay
My gastrointestinal tract wrapped
Ironically neatly around what remains
Select veins sliced-
It would be akin to electrical cords
My jaw with shattered teeth
Ripped right off of my skull
And the fingers that write these poems
All bits of bone, blood, and the grime under my nails

It's haunting, dainting, I hate it
It's all I can think about
The vision is all I see
So I move slow, snail's pace
Holding out my hand
Where are you now to hold me
Guide me through forever
My sweet feather...

The feather is what I felt
After what felt like eternity
Finally, I can see clearly
Regulate every vision, like a transformer
And it sends an electric jolt through my body
Seeing this black feather

The feather crumples into dust, and falls
Like rose petals seeping through my fingers
What is this feeling...





Oh...
It was just a bad dream...
