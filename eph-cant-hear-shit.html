CAN'T HEAR SHIT IN THE CLUB

REGRET IS KNOWING I'VE LOST MY WAY
SEARCHING FOR YOUR SIREN
IT'S SO LOUD IN HERE, VOICE TOO HARD TO HEAR
I THOUGHT I HEARD YOU, BUT YOU'RE TOO FAR
SO FRUSTRATED WITH MYSELF, MY HATRED
IT'S BLOWING ME TO SMITHEREENS
IT'S SO DAMN LOUD IN HERE
IT'S SO DAMN HARD TO HEAR
WHEN I FINALLY FIND YOU IN THE CLUB
CAN'T HEAR SHIT IN THE CLUB
