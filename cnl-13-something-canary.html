Canary Love

Poem 13

Something, Canary

You and I walked in the new city

This place has also aged extremely well
I love it here, but
I can't remember what's happened here-
I remember you, of course,
I could never forget you-
But there's gaps in my brain
It feels like my heart is filling in

I thought I was being a fool
But your love and your touch
Is the only thing on my mind
I had forgotten how obsession
Had taken over my soul

I faintly remember your rage
Rising from the ashes of my regrets
But what, God, what did I regret?
What did I do to you?
What did I do to myself?
What did I do to this place?

And how long has it been?
The hazily put together memories
Are nothing but you and flame
Is it possible that I've been put away for so long
That this place has already forgotten about me?

But that can't be possible
You've only aged a single year
This total recovery,
and then I remember

Last year, on this very day
The total destruction of this place
It was my gift to you
Darling, is it possible
You've fixed this place all by yourself?
Ca...
C...

I called you something
But now, I can't seem to remember
All I remember is your touch, your love
Your body pressed against mine
Something with a C
Something... Can-something
Something, Canary...

Ah...
I'm home, Canary
Did you miss me?
