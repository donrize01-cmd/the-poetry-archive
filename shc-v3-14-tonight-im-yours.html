Poem 14

Tonight I'm Yours (Interlude)

There's nothing between us
Except for our intertwined arms
And the blanket covering our chests

Tonight, you and I can forget everything
Tonight, I'll sleep easier than on the streets
Because tonight, I'm yours to hold
And your warmth will cradle me instead
