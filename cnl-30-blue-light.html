Canary Love

Poem 30

Blue Light

I found a gorgeous rock this morning
I was so excited to show you
I skipped all the way home
Since you called my place home now
I could skip my way to you
Like a skipping rock!

But by the time I got to you,
You also had a rock for me!
I was so happy, I was so excited
I showed you my rock
It was a pretty blue rock!
You showed me your rock
It was a pretty purple rock!
Now we have two pretty rocks
What should we do with them?

We put them up next to our blue light lamp
They look so pretty together
And they kind of remind me of us
If not for the fact that my rock is so much bigger
Than for the fact that they're together
Even after so much turmoil,
Ending up on two different shores
They found their way back to each other
