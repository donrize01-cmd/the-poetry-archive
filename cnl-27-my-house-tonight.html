Poem 27

Staying at My House Tonight

I thought you'd go home, but
My bed is really comfortable
And my arms are really warm
You could come cuddle with me
And just embrace the night
It wouldn't be the first time
And it certainly won't be the last

I know you love it here
The warmth of your hoodie-
You know, the one you took from me,
Combined with the warmth of my body
I know you get cold in your house
So why don't you stay here with me

I know how intoxicating my smell is
Don't worry, I planned on drugging you from the start
Just give in, stay at my house tonight
What's the point of going back home
If we can eat, sleep, and be together
