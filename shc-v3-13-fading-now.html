Poem 13

SURELY I'M FADING NOW BEFORE I LET MY TEARS FALL ANYWAY

EVEN NOW
EVEN WITH SOMEONE ELSE
I'LL LET MY TEARS FALL ANYWAY

I CAN'T STAND THE THOUGHT
OF ME NOT BEING IN YOUR ARMS
SEEING OTHER PEOPLE EMBRACE YOU
AND NOT GETTING THE SAME PRIVILEGE

I WANT THE WARM TOUCH
OF YOUR SKIN ON MINE

BUT I'VE GOT HER SKIN NEXT TO MINE
AND IT ISN'T AS WARM AS YOURS

I'LL WRITE THIS POEM NOW
AND REALIZE MY DELUSIONS LATER
SURELY THESE FEELINGS WILL FADE
SURELY MY HEART AND YOURS WILL FADE

I'M LETTING MY TEARS FALL ANYWAY
AND THIS GIRL IS KISSING MY FOREHEAD
CALLING ME BABYBOY
SURELY I'M FADING NOW
BEFORE I LET MY TEARS FALL ANYWAY
