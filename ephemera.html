About the Collection

Art hides in the crowds here

Ephemera is a collection of of the poems that don't make it into the collections within the Archive. It's a collection of unfinished work that desires to be seen, pieces that have no place anywhere else, and experimental poetry that challenges the stigma around poetry as a whole
