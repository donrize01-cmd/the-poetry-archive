Poem 09

Replica

so until then i'll write
a replica of your love
until i can afford to
have the real thing.

i don't have to buy
your love, not at all.
but i'd have to sell
a dangerous part

of my soul, of my
heart. a part of me
i'd previously been
unwilling to sell.
