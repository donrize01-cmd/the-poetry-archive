Poem 23

The Bridge

The bridge collapsed under my feet
It wasn't long before I hit the ground
I felt my bones shatter upon the impact
Falling bits and pieces of
Bridge and bone, animal and mine
Crushing me and finishing me
And only my heart lay intact
Under the destroyed bridge
